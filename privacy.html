Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Life Moments is a private tool run by one person, its owner, to cut the owner's videos into short clips and post them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects an account, the app receives an access token that lets it upload videos to that same account and read that account's basic profile details and post statistics. For Google, this uses the YouTube Data API.

How data is used

Access tokens and video files are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are used only to upload and schedule the owner's own clips and to read the owner's own post statistics. Text transcripts of the owner's videos are sent to an AI text service to choose clip moments and write titles. Short status messages are sent to the ntfy notification service so the owner gets alerts on their phone. Account tokens and account details are never sent to either service.

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions for Google, and in the connected apps or security settings of Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored tokens.
